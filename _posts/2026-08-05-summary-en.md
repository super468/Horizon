Vision-Language-Action models have achieved strong semantic generalization but lack explicit physical world modeling. WAMs address this limitation by integrating predictive world models into action generation, coupling imagined futures with executable robot actions.

## [China's First Mandatory L3/L4 Autonomous Driving Standard Approved, Effective 2027](https://t.me/zaihuapd/42972) ⭐️ 8.0/10

China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology has completed the approval draft for the country's first mandatory national standard for L3/L4 autonomous driving - "Safety Requirements for Autonomous Driving Systems of Intelligent Connected Vehicles." The standard introduces the Safety Case mechanism, requiring automakers to systematically demonstrate safety through a "declaration-argument-evidence" framework, with implementation scheduled for July 1, 2027. This marks a pivotal shift from "conceptual loosening" to "hard safety constraints" in autonomous vehicle regulation. Automakers can no longer rely on vague marketing claims—they must now provide rigorous, systematic safety proof. This will significantly impact how autonomous vehicles are developed, tested, and approved in China. The standard sets different requirements for L3 and L4 vehicles: L3 focuses on human-machine handover protocols, while L4 emphasizes system autonomous risk handling. The Safety Case approach, also used in international standards like UL 4600, establishes clear traceability to industry standards and enables early identification of potential safety gaps.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 4, 13:06

**Background**: SAE J3016 defines autonomous driving levels: L3 (Conditional Automation) requires a fallback-ready user who must respond to takeover requests, while L4 (High Automation) can handle all driving tasks within its operational design domain without driver intervention. Safety Case is a structured safety argument supported by evidence, increasingly used in commercial autonomous vehicles to communicate safety claims to regulators and the public.
