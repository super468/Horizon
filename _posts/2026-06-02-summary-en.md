The breakthrough was demonstrated with Dong Hui, a 39-year-old paralyzed patient from Henan province who attempted to write by holding a pen using the neural implant. It opens doors for treating spinal cord injuries and other neurological conditions, while also raising questions about human augmentation ethics. Invasive BCIs differ from non-invasive alternatives like EEG by requiring surgical implantation of microelectrode arrays directly into the brain tissue. While offering higher signal quality and precision, they carry risks of infection and biocompatibility concerns. China's Dong Hui case involved a patient with complete paralysis from the neck down due to a spinal cord injury sustained in a car accident six years earlier.

rss · MIT Technology Review · Jun 1, 12:10

**Background**: Brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) are systems that enable direct communication between the brain and external devices by bypassing traditional neural pathways. Invasive BCIs require surgery to place electrodes directly on or within the brain, offering precise neural signal recording but carrying surgical risks. Non-invasive BCIs use external sensors like EEG caps, which are safer but provide lower resolution signals. Spinal cord injuries often result in permanent paralysis because the spinal cord enables neural communication between the brain and body motor coordinates.

## [llama.cpp b9455 Adds Tensor Parallel Quantized KV Cache](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b9455) ⭐️ 7.0/10

llama.cpp release b9455 introduces tensor parallelism support for quantized KV cache, enabling significant memory footprint reduction during LLM inference across multiple platforms including macOS, Linux, Windows, Android, and iOS. This optimization benefits developers and researchers running large language models on consumer hardware by reducing memory requirements, potentially enabling longer context windows or larger models to run on devices with limited VRAM. The feature is implemented as PR #23792, allowing the KV cache to use quantization combined with tensor parallelism distribution across devices. Binaries are provided for diverse architectures including arm64, x64, Vulkan, CUDA, ROCm, OpenVINO, and HIP backends.

github · github-actions[bot] · Jun 1, 17:20

**Background**: KV cache (Key-Value cache) is a memory structure in transformer models that stores computed attention keys and values to avoid redundant calculations during autoregressive token generation. Quantization reduces the precision of stored values (e.g., from 16-bit to 2-bit) to save memory. Tensor parallelism distributes computation across multiple devices to handle larger models than a single device can accommodate.
