**Background**: Prediction markets are peer-to-peer exchanges where traders bet on the outcomes of future events, differing from centralized sports betting where all bets are placed against a bookmaker. The CFTC has authority over what constitutes a futures contract under federal law, and has historically asserted jurisdiction over prediction markets. Polymarket and similar platforms use crypto-based smart contracts to automate trades and ensure transparency.
