## [Tim Berners-Lee's 1998 Essay on URL Stability Gains renewed Attention](https://www.w3.org/Provider/Style/URI) ⭐️ 7.0/10

This 1998 essay remains highly relevant as link rot continues to plague the web, with real-world examples including broken Microsoft support articles and NSF.gov URLs, demonstrating the ongoing tension between web permanence and content churn. Berners-Lee advises against including dates, authors, subjects, or file extensions in URLs, recommending designs that can last decades. ## [49 Brain Imaging Studies Reveal COVID-19 Linked to Widespread Brain Changes](https://www.psypost.org/brain-scans-reveal-widespread-structural-and-functional-changes-in-patients-foll/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A systematic review of 49 brain imaging studies published in Cerebral Cortex reveals that COVID-19 infection is associated with widespread structural and functional brain changes, including gray matter volume reduction, altered cortical thickness, and white matter microstructure abnormalities in regions related to emotion, memory, and executive function. This meta-analysis provides valuable aggregated evidence about COVID-19's neurological impact, which is crucial for understanding long COVID and its effects on cognitive and emotional health. The findings could inform clinical monitoring and rehabilitation strategies for patients experiencing post-COVID neurological symptoms. Functional MRI studies showed abnormal spontaneous brain activity and functional connectivity, with structural or functional abnormalities also reported in the insula, hippocampus, and amygdala. Some studies correlated these imaging findings with brain fog, fatigue, memory performance, and mood. Notably, even mild cases showed cerebral blood flow abnormalities and white matter microstructure changes.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 10, 00:02

**Background**: Gray matter volume (GMV) is a key metric in neuroimaging that reflects the amount of neuronal cell bodies in brain tissue, often measured using voxel-based morphometry (VBM) with MRI. White matter microstructure is typically assessed using diffusion tensor imaging (DTI), which can detect subtle changes in the brain's fiber tracts. Functional MRI (fMRI) measures brain activity through blood oxygen level-dependent (BOLD) signals, revealing functional connectivity patterns between brain regions.
