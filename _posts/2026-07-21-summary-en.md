## [AI Claude Fable Finds Jacobian Conjecture Counterexample](https://xcancel.com/__alpoge__/status/2079028340955197566) ⭐️ 10.0/10

Claude Fable, an AI assistant developed by Anthropic, has produced a counterexample to the Jacobian Conjecture, a famous unsolved problem in algebraic geometry that has stood for over 85 years. This is a groundbreaking mathematical breakthrough. The Jacobian Conjecture was number 16 in Stephen Smale's 1998 list of Mathematical Problems for the Next Century, and has defeated countless mathematicians who attempted to prove or disprove it. A counterexample proves the conjecture is false, saving countless hours of future research efforts. The counterexample found by Claude Fable is in degree 7, which is surprisingly low compared to previous estimates. Some researchers had previously estimated that counterexamples might require degrees up to 200 using brute-force searches in 16 variables.

hackernews · loubbrad · Jul 20, 02:51

**Background**: The Jacobian Conjecture states that if a polynomial function from n-dimensional space to itself has a Jacobian determinant that is a non-zero constant, then the function has a polynomial inverse. The conjecture was first stated for two variables in 1884 by Ludwig Kraus and restated for integer-coefficient polynomials in n variables in 1939 by Ott-Heinrich Keller. For the special case of two variables (N=2), the conjecture remains unsolved as of 2026.

The vulnerability affects Fastjson 1.2.68-1.2.83 and works without autoTypeSupport enabled, unlike most Fastjson deserialization flaws that require this feature. Since Fastjson 1.x is EOL, the only recommended remediation is upgrading to Fastjson2 or enabling SafeMode in startup parameters and configuration files.
